Fair questions

The things people actually want to know

Walify connects via Plaid — the same bank-linking technology used by thousands of financial apps. It's read-only: we can see your transactions and balances, nothing else. We cannot log into your bank, initiate transfers, or touch your money. Your username and password never touch Walify's servers, ever.
Wally is powered by Claude Opus 4.6 — one of the most capable AI models available — with adaptive thinking enabled. That means before it responds, it reads your actual transaction history, income, expenses, and debt, then reasons through your specific situation. It's not keyword matching or scripts. It genuinely thinks about your question in the context of your real financial data.
Not at all. Bank sync is optional. You can use Walify entirely by entering transactions manually — the Free plan fully supports this. Plaid bank sync is available on Free (1 connection) and unlimited on Pro, but it's always your choice.
Plaid supports 12,000+ financial institutions — Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citi, US Bank, and the vast majority of credit unions. If your bank has online banking, it almost certainly works.
The Free plan is genuinely free — no credit card, no 14-day trial, no expiry. You get manual tracking, budgeting, goals, debt optimizer, 3 reports, and 5 Wally AI conversations per month. The only catch is you want to use it more. Pro ($9.99/mo) unlocks unlimited banks, 50 AI conversations/day, all 12 reports, CSV/PDF import, and business mode.
Yes — cancel in one click from the billing portal, no phone calls required. If you cancel, you stay on Pro until the end of your billing period, then drop to Free. Your data is never deleted. You can export everything anytime from Settings → Backup & Restore.
