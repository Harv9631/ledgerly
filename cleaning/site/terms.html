We arrange residential cleaning services — including recurring home cleaning, deep cleans, move-in/move-out cleans, and short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO) turnovers — in Ponte Vedra, Nocatee, the Jacksonville beaches, and surrounding areas of St. Johns and Duval counties. 4. Booking, rescheduling & cancellation

  • You may reschedule or cancel any visit free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice by phone, text, or email.

  • Arrival windows are estimates. Traffic, weather, and prior jobs can shift timing; we'll keep you posted if a team is running late.

You're responsible for providing access at the scheduled time — whether you're home, leave a key or code, or use a lockbox. Any access instructions you give us are shared only with the team assigned to your job. Please secure valuables, firearms, and irreplaceable items before each visit.

6. Satisfaction guarantee

If something was missed, tell us within 24 hours of your cleaning and we'll send a team back to make it right at no charge. The guarantee covers re-cleaning the areas in question; it is not a right to a refund for services performed, though we may offer refunds or credits at our discretion.

7. Payment

  • Payment is due at or after each completed visit, charged to the card on file or invoiced through our client portal.

  • Late or failed payments may result in paused service. Returned payments may incur bank fees.
  • Tips are never required. If you choose to tip, 100% goes to your cleaning team.

  • We are not responsible for damage resulting from pre-existing conditions (loose fixtures, improperly hung items, worn finishes), inherently fragile items not disclosed in advance, or normal wear.

9. Safety & service limits

For everyone's safety, cleaning teams do not: clean biohazards (mold remediation, animal or human waste beyond routine bathroom cleaning, pest infestations), move furniture or items heavier than about 25 lbs, climb higher than a two-step stool, clean exterior windows above ground level, or handle firearms. Please secure pets that may be anxious or protective during visits. Homes must have working utilities (water and electricity) at the time of service.
