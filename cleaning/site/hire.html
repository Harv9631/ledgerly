Why teams join

Recurring homes, not one-offs

Most of our work is weekly and bi-weekly homes — the same addresses on your calendar every week, no chasing new customers or paying for ads. Your ratings decide how much work flows to you.

How pay works

Transparent per-job pay

You see the job price before you accept it, and your share is the majority of it — paid out every week by direct deposit. No fees to join, no deductions surprises, no waiting on invoices.

Your business

You stay independent

You're an independent business, not an employee — your supplies, your methods, your right to accept or decline any job, and no exclusivity. We're a customer source, not a boss.

  • Residential cleaning experience (2+ years preferred) — or impress us on a paid trial clean
